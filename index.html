🏝️ My World

A personal, self-hosted life dashboard for one person.

My World is a small 3D "town" that runs on its owner's own computer (and later a personal Arduino UNO Q board). It keeps track of the owner's internship applications, class deadlines, to-dos, habits and workouts.

What it does with Gmail

  • It requests read-only access to the owner's own Gmail (gmail.readonly).
  • It looks only at recruiting-related messages (application confirmations, assessments, interviews, offers, rejections) and reads the sender, subject, date and Gmail's short preview snippet.
  • From that, it updates the owner's own application tracker. It never sends, deletes, labels or changes any email.
  • Everything stays on the owner's own device. Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else.

Who can use it

It's a personal tool. It isn't offered as a service, and the only account that uses it is its owner's.
