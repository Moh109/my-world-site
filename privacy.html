Privacy Policy

My World · effective October 28, 2026

My World is a personal, self-hosted application used only by its owner. This policy explains what it does with Google user data.

Data accessed

  • Gmail, read-only (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly). For recruiting-related messages only: sender name and address, subject, date, and Gmail's short preview snippet.
  • Full message bodies and attachments are not stored.

How the data is used

  • Only to keep the owner's own internship-application tracker up to date (e.g. marking an application as "Applied", "Online Assessment", "Interview", "Offer" or "Rejected").
  • My World never sends, deletes, modifies or labels email, and never acts on the owner's behalf outside the app.
  • No advertising, analytics, profiling or selling of data.

Storage and sharing

  • All data is stored only on the owner's own device (a personal computer or personal single-board computer), in a local database and local files. It is not uploaded to any server operated by the developer.
  • Google user data is not shared with or transferred to any third party.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally, excluded from source control, and never logged.

Limited Use

My World's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

  • The app's activity history is kept for 90 days, then deleted automatically. Application records stay until the owner removes them.
  • The owner can delete everything at any time with the app's "Reset world" option or by deleting its local data folder.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
